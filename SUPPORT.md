## Questions and bugs

Use the GitHub issue tracker:

<https://github.com/spacelibres/STKCraft-time/issues>

Choose the bug report or feature request template and include:

- the crate version and enabled features;
- whether `std`, `no_std` or `mcp` is enabled;
- the LSK, SCLK or EOP data source (do not attach restricted data);
- a minimal reproduction and the observed result.

## Security

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
